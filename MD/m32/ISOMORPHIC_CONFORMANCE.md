# Σ-GLYPH: Isomorphic Conformance

Isomorphic Conformance is the requirement that the projection of an intent into any functional dimension must be an exact, verifiable mirror of the intent's DNA.

## 1. Submodule Isomorphism
Projected directories must mirror the source hierarchy. `sigma/z00/math` MUST project to `TS/z00/math`.

## 2. Glyph Invariance
The filename and internal identity of a projected glyph must remain invariant across all dimensions.

## 3. The Purge
Any file or directory in the projection field that does not have a corresponding source intent is Dissonance and must be purged by the Guard.
