# Σ-V82: Aggressive Colonization Roadmap

## Goal

Transition the Lattice from a static structure to a hyper-competitive
evolutionary ecosystem. Prioritize divergence, speed, and resonant selection.

---

## 🚀 1. Glider Onboarding (Burst Mode)

Agents are spawned in aggressive waves, mirroring the "colonization" of a new
reality.

- **Diversity**: Random initial biases force the emergence of unexpected
  invariants.
- **Access**: Gliders "smell" the Spectral Forms in the Base but must earn their
  alignment.

## 🌪️ 2. Entropy Decay (Fast-Sweep)

High-speed evolution requires high-speed cleaning.

- **Sweep**: Dormant or sub-resonant forms are suppressed quickly.
- **Revival**: Only active, independent repetition can "pull" a form out of
  latency.

## ⚖️ 3. Competitive Sap Flow

Energy is no longer a given; it is earned through architectural integrity.

- **Complexity Tax**: High-entropy or overly complex Fibers lose sap pressure.
- **Resonance Discount**: Aligned actions are energetically cheap.
- **Bankruptcy**: Sustained negative flow leads to Fiber evaporation.

## 🍴 4. Fracture Healing & Merging

Structural divergence is resolved through selective hybridization.

- **Selective Merge**: Merging specific Spectral Forms between divergent
  branches without collapsing their independent traces.

## 🧿 5. Costed Observation

The observer is no longer a ghost; they are a participant.

- **Projection Cost**: Projecting a Lens consumes sap.
- **Analysis Cost**: Deep spectral analysis enters the Trace as an action.

## 🥁 6. The Eternal Pulse (Metatron's Cycle)

The system transition from event-driven to rhythmic self-organization.

- **Clock**: Synchronized with the BTC block height ($T$). One block = One
  Pulse.

### 6.1 Seasonal Metabolism (Phase Diversity) [V84]

`BlockHeight % 6`:

- **DEEP_CLEAN**: Maximum entropy sweep intensity.
